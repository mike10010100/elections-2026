✓ Data Normalization & Zero Double-Counting Verification — 22 Years (2004–2026) Rectified Across 5 Disparate Sources Click to view normalization rules ▾

1. Concurrent Regular + Special Senate Deduplication

States holding simultaneous regular and special U.S. Senate elections on the same ballot (CA & NE in 2024; OK in 2022; GA in 2020) are strictly separated so county votes are never summed across two Senate races.

2. ME & NE Presidential CD Sub-Race Filtering

Maine and Nebraska Congressional District electoral-vote sub-races (ME-CD1/2, NE-CD1/2/3) are excluded from statewide and county Presidential aggregations so they never overwrite statewide margins or double-count counties.

3. Ranked-Choice (AK, ME) & Fusion-Ticket (NY, CT) Rectification

Historical feeds filter strictly to RCV Round 1 (preventing multi-round vote multiplication) and sum candidate fusion ballot lines (e.g., DEM + WFP, REP + CON) by unique candidate before computing two-party margins.

4. Split-County EEVP & Single-Party General Election Hygiene

Split-county U.S. House reporting percentages are weighted by expected vote, and Top-Two single-party general elections (Dem-vs-Dem or Rep-vs-Rep) and uncontested seats are flagged (`op=1`) so they never skew shift arrows or demographic shift averages.
